\# Measure integration quality Integration quality measurement is a certification process that ensures your implementation meets the security and quality requirements needed to offer the best experience with Mercado Pago for both the seller and the buyer. Therefore, before going to production, we recommend that you complete this step. Below you will find the aspects used in this analysis and how to use the tool to maintain a continuous improvement process. > NOTE > > This documentation applies \*\*only\*\* to integrations with \*\*Checkout API via Orders\*\*. For other payment solutions, see their respective quality measurement sections. :::AccordionComponent{title="Aspects evaluated in the quality measurement"} The measurement process analyzes a set of fields associated with fundamental aspects that a Mercado Pago integration must have. You can see these evaluated attributes below: | Aspects | Description | |---|---| | Buyer experience | To grow and maintain your user base, you must offer a good payment experience. The measurement suggestions guide you to achieve the best results. | | Financial reconciliation | Consistent financial verification of transactions with Mercado Pago maintains data integrity in your system. It is important to implement the measures and best practices indicated in the result. | | Payment approval | For a good approval rate, validate the fields flagged as required improvements and implement the best practices. This provides more information to our fraud prevention tools for a more detailed assessment. | | Scalability | Make sure to use the latest versions of our APIs and official libraries to get the best results. | | Security | We aim to ensure the confidentiality of the data involved in the purchase. The improvements and best practices indicated in the result allow you to obtain and use the data securely. | ::: :::::AccordionComponent{title="Measure the quality of your integration"} To perform the measurement, you need to declare your integration data. To do this, follow the instructions below: 1\. Locate your application configured with \*\*Checkout API via Orders\*\* and, from the \*\*">"\*\* button, select it. !\[Configurations\](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/images/api-orders/tusintegraciones-v1.png) 2\. Go to the \*\*Test your integration\*\* step. !\[Configurations\](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/images/api-orders/datosdeintegracion-v1.png) 3\. Declare the requested information: \* \*\*How the integration will operate\*\*: state the brands you will use, the countries where operations will be carried out, and the Mercado Pago accounts used. \* \*\*Payment methods offered\*\*: declare the payment methods that will be enabled to receive payments with the integration, whether cards, transfers, or offline methods. \* \*\*Other integrated functionalities\*\*: indicate whether additional modules and advanced security options will be integrated, such as 3DS. Once you have completed the declaration with your integration information, you can perform the quality measurement in two ways: \*\*manual\*\* or \*\*automatic\*\*. > NOTE > > Quality measurement is only available when your integration in the test environment already has activity. Before starting, make sure you have completed development and run at least one test payment. ::::TabsComponent :::TabComponent{title="Manual measurement"} If you prefer, you can perform a manual evaluation of your integration whenever you have an \*\*Order ID\*\* from a test payment. This is useful for new integrations that want to test their implementation before going to production and validate that it meets the required quality and security requirements. To manually measure the quality of your integration in the test environment, go back to the \[Integration data\](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/developers/en/docs/checkout-api-orders/resources/integration-data) screen for the application and follow the steps below: 1\. Select \*\*Test your integration\*\* and click \*\*Start\*\*. !\[Configurations\](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/images/api-orders/comenzarprobarintegracion-v1.png) 2\. In the \*\*Measure the quality of your integration\*\* step, click \*\*Start measurement\*\*. !\[Configurations\](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/images/api-orders/medirlacalidad-v1.png) 3\. Enter the \*\*Order ID of the test payment\*\*. It must correspond to a payment made within the \*\*last 7 days\*\* with :toolTipComponent\[test credentials\]{link="/developers/en/docs/checkout-api-orders/resources/credentials" linkText="Credentials" content="Unique access keys we use to identify an integration in your account, linked to your application. For more information, access the link below."}. Then click \*\*Measure quality\*\*. !\[Configurations\](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/images/api-orders/orderid-v1.png) 4\. Once the \*\*Order ID\*\* has been evaluated, you will see the quality measurement result. The result can be: !\[Configurations\](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/images/api-orders/resultadopanel-v1.png) | Result | Explanation | |---|---| | Below ideal | Your integration requires necessary actions to meet the expected quality. | | Close to ideal | Your integration meets the requirements but can still improve. | | Ideal | Your integration meets all expected requirements. | 5\. Click \*\*Go to details\*\* to see the \*\*quality of your integration\*\*. In this panel you will find the evaluation result with the information broken down into \*\*requirements\*\*, \*\*recommendations\*\*, and \*\*best practices\*\* so you can identify what to adjust and, if necessary, apply changes and \*\*run the measurement again\*\*. !\[Configurations\](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/images/api-orders/resultadohomologator-v1.png) > NOTE > > In the left side menu you will always have the \*\*Evaluation\*\* section available, where you can modify your integration information and perform new evaluations. In this way, you will have completed the quality measurement manually. To continue your integration process and go to production, click the \*\*Next\*\* button and activate your :toolTipComponent\[production credentials\]{link="/developers/en/docs/checkout-api-orders/resources/credentials" linkText="Credentials" content="Set of unique access keys linked to your application that allow you to receive real payments. You can access them through \*Your integrations > Integration data > Production > Production credentials\*. Use your production Public Key on the frontend and your production Access Token on the backend. For more information, access the link below."}. ::: :::TabComponent{title="Automatic measurement"} Mercado Pago performs an automatic evaluation of all applications integrated with \*\*Checkout API via Orders\*\*. The automatic measurement process is carried out \*\*monthly\*\*, evaluating the first productive transaction of the month, even when you have previously performed a manual measurement. In this way, we ensure that your integration remains aligned with best practices over time. You can find the results of this automatic measurement in \[Integration data\](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/developers/en/docs/checkout-api-orders/resources/integration-data). As with manual measurement, you can view your application's score, the actions indicated as necessary, and the suggested best practices. For more details, see the section \[Analyze the results of the measurement\](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/developers/en/docs/checkout-api-orders/integration-quality#bookmark\_analyze\_the\_results\_of\_the\_measurement). ::: :::: ::::: :::AccordionComponent{title="Analyze the results of the measurement"} Whether you have performed a manual quality measurement or received the results of your automatic evaluation, in \[Integration data\](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/developers/en/docs/checkout-api-orders/resources/integration-data) for the application, in the \*\*Evaluation\*\* section of the menu, you will find the following information: 1\. \*\*Score\*\*: indicates how secure your application configuration is and whether it is aligned with Mercado Pago integration best practices. The minimum score for your application to meet the requirements is \*\*73\*\*, but we recommend achieving \*\*100 points\*\* to improve the user experience and increase the payment approval rate. 2\. \*\*Last measurement date and Order ID\*\*: indicates the day and time of the last measurement and the \*\*Order ID\*\* on which the application's quality score is based. 3\. \*\*Evaluated aspects\*\*: indicates the score obtained for each of the evaluated aspects. Select each one to see what improvement opportunities were identified in the process and how to address them. You can also filter by requirements, recommendations, and best practices. See the section \[Actions to improve the quality of your integration\](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/developers/en/docs/checkout-api-orders/integration-quality#bookmark\_actions\_to\_improve\_the\_quality\_of\_your\_integration) for more optimization options. 4\. \*\*Measure again\*\*: once you have applied the improvement opportunities, you can measure the quality of your integration again manually or, if you prefer, wait for the monthly automatic measurement performed by Mercado Pago. ::: :::AccordionComponent{title="Actions to improve the quality of your integration"} As a result of the quality measurement of your integration, our tool will point out different items that will allow you to optimize its performance and improve the experience for both the seller and the buyer. !\[Configurations\](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/images/api-orders/resultadohomologator-v1.png) These actions can be: \* \*\*Mandatory actions\*\*: requirements you must meet to ensure integration quality and improve your score. For example, enabling \[Webhooks\](https://www.mercadopago.com.ar/developers/en/docs/checkout-api-orders/notifications) notifications or sending an external reference that allows correlating payments between Mercado Pago and your system. \* \*\*Recommended actions\*\*: tasks that improve the score but do not prevent you from certifying the integration. For example, sending all buyer information to improve payment validation and security and reduce rejections by the fraud prevention engine. \* \*\*Best practices\*\*: recommendations that do not affect the score but improve aspects of your integration. For example, adding the MercadoPago.js V2 SDKs to your project or keeping your system up to date according to the events received via notifications. Actions and best practices depend on each integration. Although information is provided here for Checkout API via Orders, you should be guided by the information returned by the tool in the quality measurement result to optimize the operation of your integration and your score. :::
